# POSITIONS

Standing judgments. Tier 2: one other member reviews, the operator merges.

Each position has a number, the judgment in one or two sentences, the evidence with a source from `KNOWLEDGE/SOURCES.md`, and "Last changed" with a date. A changed position keeps its number and gets a new date. A dropped position is struck through, not deleted.
